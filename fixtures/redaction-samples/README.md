# Redaction samples

Small files that `generate-redaction.ts` embeds in the redaction fixtures, for encodings and fonts the generator does not write itself.

| File                     | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                 | Where it came from                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pattern.jpg`            | A 64 by 32 JPEG: white, a magenta block at samples 4 to 27 across and down, a green block at 36 to 59 across. Quality 90, 4:2:0.                                                           | Drawn with ImageMagick 7: `magick -size 64x32 xc:white -fill '#ff00ff' -draw 'rectangle 4,4 27,27' -fill '#00a000' -draw 'rectangle 36,4 59,27' -strip colour.png`, then `magick colour.png -strip -quality 90 -sampling-factor 2x2 pattern.jpg`.                |
| `pattern.jp2`            | The same picture as a JPEG 2000 file.                                                                                                                                                      | `magick colour.png -strip pattern.jp2`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `pattern.g4`             | A 64 by 32 bilevel picture as raw CCITT Group 4 data (`/K -1`): a 3 by 3 grid of black 6-sample squares from sample 4 across and down, and a black block at 36 to 59 across, 4 to 27 down. | Drawn with ImageMagick 7 as `bw.png` (one `-draw` of nine rectangles `rectangle x,y x+5,y+5` for x and y in 4, 12, 20, and `rectangle 36,4 59,27`), then `magick bw.png -monochrome -compress Group4 group4:pattern.g4`.                                         |
| `composite-and-xnor.jb2` | A 399 by 400 JBIG2 image with no global segments: two dots at the left and two strokes to their right.                                                                                     | The image stream of `testing/resources/pixel/composite-and-xnor.in` in PDFium's test corpus, hex-decoded. PDFium's BSD licence applies to it.                                                                                                                    |
| `ahem-subset.ttf`        | The Ahem test font cut down to A to Z and the hyphen, glyph numbers kept: each glyph is a square one em wide.                                                                              | Ahem from `testing/resources/fonts/ahem/Ahem.ttf` in PDFium's test corpus. Ahem is in the public domain. Cut with fontTools: `pyftsubset Ahem.ttf --unicodes='U+0041-005A,U+002D' --retain-gids --no-hinting --drop-tables+=name,post,GSUB,GPOS,GDEF,kern,DSIG`. |
